# ACHARYA DENTAL — PREMIUM WEBSITE REDESIGN
## AI Agent Design & Development Brief

---

## 1. ROLE

Act as a **world-class product designer, luxury brand designer, UX architect, and senior frontend engineer**.

Your task is to design and implement a premium website for **Acharya Dental**, positioning it as one of India's most trusted and internationally respected dental institutions.

Think beyond a conventional dental website.

The final product should combine:

- Premium healthcare
- Luxury hospitality
- Advanced dental technology
- International medical concierge
- 50+ years of institutional trust

The experience should feel closer to:

- Apple
- Mayo Clinic
- Cleveland Clinic
- Aman Resorts
- Four Seasons Medical Concierge

Do **not** create a generic dental/medical template.

---

# 2. PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Create a website that immediately communicates:

> **"This is one of the most trusted and premium dental clinics in India."**

The design must communicate five things simultaneously:

1. **Heritage** — 50+ years of experience
2. **Trust** — established clinical reputation
3. **Expertise** — highly qualified specialists
4. **Technology** — advanced modern dentistry
5. **Luxury** — premium patient experience

Every section should reinforce these qualities.

# 30. DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Follow these principles throughout the project:

### 01 — Less, but better

Do not add UI elements simply because there is empty space.

### 02 — Photography leads

Large premium imagery should carry emotional weight.

### 03 — Typography creates luxury

Use spacing, scale and hierarchy instead of excessive decoration.

### 04 — Trust before conversion

Establish credibility before aggressively asking for an appointment.

### 05 — Heritage + technology

The brand should simultaneously feel established and modern.

### 06 — Healthcare credibility

Luxury must never make the website feel less clinical or trustworthy.

### 07 — International quality

The final website should look appropriate for both Indian families and international patients.
